## Proof by Contrapositive

For any conditional to be true, its contrapositive must also be true. So, $\p\to q$ and $\neg q \to \neg p$ are both valid statements. For example, "if it's raining, then I will bring my umbrella" and "if I didn't bring my umbrella, then it is not raining".

These contrapositives might be easier to prove than the originals. If we wanted to prove "if $x^2$ is odd, then $x$ is odd$ that might prove difficult. The contrapositive, on the other hand, it much more straightforward. "If $x$ is even, then $x^2$ is even".
